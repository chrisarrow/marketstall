Privacy Policy

Market Stall · Effective 10 October 2026

The short version

Market Stall collects no data. Nothing you enter leaves your phone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network connections.

What stays on your phone

  • Your stall. Your items, prices, stock counts, payment handles and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your device.
  • Your sales. Each sale you mark as paid (items, amounts, time and payment method) is saved on your device so the Today screen can total your day.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Payments

Market Stall never handles money or payment details. The QR code it shows is a link to your own payment profile. When a customer scans it, their payment app takes over, under that company's own privacy policy.

Sharing

Data leaves the app only when you choose to export a CSV of your sales through the share sheet. Where it goes is up to you.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
